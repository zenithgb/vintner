# Phase 7A — Estate Registration Acceptance

Estate identity begins at the existing Vintage Archive, keeping registration
inside a physical winery workflow rather than a detached menu.

## Founding an estate

1. Craft a Vintner's Almanac and rename it in an anvil to the desired estate
   name.
2. Optionally hold a banner in the other hand; its base colour becomes the
   estate crest colour.
3. Sneak-use the named Almanac on any Vintage Archive.
4. The world records the founder, estate name, vineyard name, bottle label,
   founding year, home biome, archive location, and crest colour.
5. Rename the Almanac and repeat the interaction to rename the estate. The
   original founding year, home region, and crest remain unless a new banner
   is supplied.

## Using the identity

- Sneak-use the Almanac in the air to review the registered identity.
- Pressed batches made by the founder inherit the registered estate name.
- Fermentation, ageing, bottling, archives, racks, and crates preserve that
  metadata through the existing wine provenance path.
- Players without a registered estate retain the existing independent-vineyard
  naming behavior.

## Manual checks

1. Rename an Almanac to `Stone Hill Estate` and hold a blue banner offhand.
2. Sneak-use it on a Vintage Archive and confirm the founding message includes
   the current vintage year and biome region.
3. Sneak-use the Almanac in the air and confirm the estate summary lists the
   derived vineyard name, bottle label, and blue crest.
4. Press four grapes and inspect the must with an Almanac; it should identify
   `Stone Hill Estate`.
5. Complete fermentation, ageing, bottling, and storage, confirming that the
   same estate identity remains.
6. Rename the Almanac and update the estate without an offhand banner. Confirm
   the name changes while the founding year, region, and blue crest remain.
7. Repeat pressing with an unregistered player and confirm the legacy default
   naming behavior is unchanged.

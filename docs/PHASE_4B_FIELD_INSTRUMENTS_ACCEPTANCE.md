# Phase 4B acceptance — Field Instruments and Records

Phase 4B keeps vineyard inspection compact and vanilla-like by treating the
Vintner's Almanac as a field book with context-aware pages rather than adding a
row of redundant one-purpose tools.

## Roadmap mapping

- Climate journal: use the Almanac on recognized vineyard ground or a trellis.
- Soil profile: use the Soil Probe for a quick reading or the Almanac for the
  complete site report.
- Ripeness tester: use the Almanac on either half of a grapevine.
- Hydrometer: use the Almanac on a Fermentation Barrel.
- Cellar thermometer: use the Almanac on an Aging Barrel or existing cellar
  storage.
- Tasting notebook: hold the Almanac and a wine bottle in opposite hands, then
  use the Almanac.
- Cooperage reference: sneak-use the Almanac on an Aging Barrel.
- Vineyard survey map: sneak-use the Almanac on a recognized site to bookmark
  its location and summary; use the Almanac in the air without a bottle to
  recall it. The foldout record lives in the field book rather than occupying a
  second inventory slot.

## Automated acceptance

- Almanac target routing distinguishes vineyard sites, vines, fermentation,
  ageing, and unrelated blocks.
- Fermentation and ageing reports expose their real fill state, progress, and
  remaining process time without modifying inventory.
- Unrelated construction blocks do not produce misleading terroir reports.
- Existing survey and wine-inspection advancements remain one-time awards.
- Survey bookmarks persist through item copies and save/load data components.
- The complete build, asset audit, and GameTest suite pass.

## Manual acceptance

1. Use the Almanac on vineyard soil, ordinary recognized soil, and a trellis.
2. Use it on young and ripe lower and upper grapevine halves.
3. Inspect empty, partially filled, processing, and ready fermentation barrels.
4. Repeat for every Aging Barrel treatment and compare cellar environments.
5. Sneak-use an Aging Barrel to open its cooperage guidance.
6. Use the Almanac on an unrelated decorative block and confirm it declines to
   invent a reading.
7. Inspect a bottle by holding it opposite the Almanac.

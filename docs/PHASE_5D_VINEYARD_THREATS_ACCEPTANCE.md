# Phase 5D — Vineyard Threats Acceptance

Vineyard threats are deterministic seasonal pressures, not random block loss.
The Almanac reports one prioritized condition and its counterplay, avoiding
maintenance spam while making poor growing conditions affect vintage quality.

## Manual acceptance

1. Inspect a vine on ordinary dirt. The Almanac should report Nutrient
   Imbalance and recommend compost.
2. Inspect prepared vineyard soil during a drought. Without nearby water it
   should report Drought Stress; a water channel within four blocks mitigates
   the seasonal event and restores the healthy reading.
3. During late frost, overhead glass should mitigate the threat.
4. Heavy rain on ripe fruit should report Rot Risk and recommend harvesting.
5. Heavy rain on an unripe, humid, poorly ventilated site should report Mildew
   Risk and recommend better airflow.
6. Heatwaves should report Heat Stress and recommend controlled irrigation.
7. Threatened vines remain planted. Their condition contribution falls below
   the healthy six-point maximum instead of deleting crops or repeatedly
   interrupting the player.

The existing seasonal outlook, protection, irrigation, soil preparation, and
harvest timing are the counterplay. Pest, bird, netting, and resistant-rootstock
systems remain later Phase 5 slices rather than pretending those counters exist.

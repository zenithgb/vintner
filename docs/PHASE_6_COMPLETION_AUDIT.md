# Phase 6 — Table and Pantry Completion Audit

Phase 6 now provides a complete loop for current Vintner wines: production
creates useful byproducts, wines have differentiated benefits, the pantry
turns vineyard goods into meals, compatible installations gain optional
Farmer's Delight recipes, and shared meals create a multiplayer feast benefit.

## Delivered scope

| Roadmap area | Delivered |
| --- | --- |
| 6.1 Wine benefits | Distinct current red and white wine profiles, food pairing, consumption limits, and expanded age and quality scaling. |
| 6.2 Cooking | Red wine stew, braised meat, white wine fish, vineyard soup, poached fruit, vineyard bread, grape tart, and raisins. |
| 6.3 Farmer's Delight | Optional cutting-board and cooking-pot recipes with no mandatory dependency. |
| 6.4 Group feasts | Shared morale and wine-duration bonuses around a Tasting Cabinet for two or more diners. |
| 6.5 Byproducts | Pomace compost, animal feed, dye, and trade uses; grape-seed oil, feed, and trade uses; cutting compost and fuel uses. |
| Interoperability | Conventional food-category tags and reusable bowls for bowl-based meals. |

## Intentionally deferred

- Mulled wine waits for a dedicated hot-drink product and presentation pass.
- Wine sauce waits for the broader sauce and recipe family rather than adding
  a one-off placeholder.
- Vinegar and vinegar pickles remain part of the dedicated vinegar production
  milestone scheduled later in the roadmap.
- Pomace distillation waits for the spirits and distillation system.
- Decorative cutting bundles wait for a deliberate model and art pass.
- Advanced wine families keep their own later production and release
  milestones rather than being simulated with renamed current bottles.

These deferrals preserve the release plan and avoid shipping recipe-only
objects without a coherent production loop or finished art.

## Release boundary

Automated validation can establish source and compatibility readiness, but
manual in-game acceptance, release integration, version metadata, tagging, and
publication remain separate steps.

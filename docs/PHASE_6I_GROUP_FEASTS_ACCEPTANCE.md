# Phase 6I — Group Feasts Acceptance

Group feasts reuse the existing Tasting Cabinet as a serving anchor. This
keeps the feature visually grounded in the cellar furniture players already
understand instead of adding a second table-shaped workstation.

## Rules

- A player must complete a valid food-and-wine pairing within four blocks of
  any wood variant of Tasting Cabinet.
- At least one other player must be within eight blocks.
- Everyone nearby receives four minutes of feast morale.
- Feast morale reduces general and combat exhaustion by 15%.
- Any active wine profile on a diner is extended by 15% when the feast begins.
- A diner cannot repeatedly restart the same feast while their morale is
  active.
- Solo dining and paired dining away from a Tasting Cabinet behave exactly as
  before.

## Manual checks

1. Place a Tasting Cabinet and gather two players within eight blocks.
2. Have one player drink a wine and eat a correctly paired meal.
3. Confirm both players receive the short shared-feast overlay message.
4. Sprint and fight during the four-minute window; hunger should drain a
   little more slowly without feeling like a permanent buff.
5. Repeat the pairing immediately and confirm the feast does not stack.
6. Repeat the same meal and wine with one player, then away from the cabinet;
   neither case should start a feast.
7. Confirm every Tasting Cabinet wood variant works as the anchor.

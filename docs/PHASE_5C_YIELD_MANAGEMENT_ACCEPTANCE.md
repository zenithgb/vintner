# Phase 5C — Yield Management Acceptance

Every established two-block grapevine can retain one pruning strategy across
growth, harvests, reloads, and grafting.

## Manual acceptance

1. Train a grapevine until both halves reach fruit set or ripe growth.
2. Use shears on the lower trunk. The strategy should move from Balanced to
   Quality Focus, then High Yield, then back to Balanced.
3. Each change consumes one shears durability in Survival and none in Creative.
4. Changing a ripe vine's strategy performs a green harvest: both halves return
   to fruit set and must ripen again.
5. Inspect the vine with the Vintner's Almanac. It should show the selected
   strategy, harvest adjustment, and quality contribution.
6. Harvest comparable vines in all three modes. High Yield gives two extra
   grapes, Quality Focus gives one fewer grape and up to two extra quality
   points, and Balanced retains the established output.
7. Graft a managed vine. Its variety changes but its yield strategy remains.
8. Break the vine completely and plant a new one in the same location. The new
   root should begin in Balanced mode.

Using shears on the ripe upper canopy still takes a cutting. The lower trunk is
the explicit yield-management target, keeping propagation and pruning distinct.

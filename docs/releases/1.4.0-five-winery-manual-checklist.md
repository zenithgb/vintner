# Vintner 1.4.0 exact-RC manual checklist

Use the exact candidate SHA-256 recorded in `1.4.0-release-state.md`.
Record world seed, village coordinates, rotation/road side, screenshots,
observed result, and candidate checksum. No item below is pre-accepted.

## Five generated village wineries

- [ ] Plains: inspect the original player winery and vineyard, with the raised
  road connector and unchanged visible build.
- [ ] Desert: inspect the player tasting-house/winery, vineyard, and reachable
  Jungle Grape Press.
- [ ] Savanna: inspect the player winery and separate vineyard together; judge
  road connection and the separation distance.
- [ ] Snowy: inspect the player tavern/winery and protected vineyard, including
  snow/terrain fit and the Spruce Grape Press.
- [ ] Taiga: inspect the player winery and separate vineyard together; judge
  road connection and the separation distance.
- [ ] Across all five: check architectural preservation, foundation/terrain,
  all encountered rotations, complete vines/trellises, doors and paths, clipping,
  visible models/textures, and ordinary Winemaker behavior.

## Existing current-RC gameplay delta

- [ ] Run the existing current-RC visual and ordinary gameplay route: cultivars,
  planting/growth, propagation, processing, fermentation, ageing, bottles,
  tasting, storage, workstations, Estate Desk/Atlas, recipes, and notifications.
- [ ] Test with two real players on the exact RC: ownership isolation,
  simultaneous interactions, save/reconnect, and unload/reload.
- [ ] Upgrade only a copy of a genuine public 1.3.1 world; compare persisted
  wine, machines, storage, and supported estate data before/after restart.

These are Zach's manual acceptance gates. Record failures before publication;
the automated suite and technical client/server startup do not mark them passed.

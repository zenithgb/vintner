### Scoped implementation

- Wine Racks and Wine Crates derive a compact red, white or mixed display state
  from their canonical stored bottle stacks. Capacity, ordering, retrieval,
  comparison output, ageing, persistence and exact item components remain
  authoritative in the existing block entities.
- Placed wine now derives its red/white state from `WineMetadata.wineStyle`,
  rather than a fixed item list. Its existing canonical player-facing name and
  full bottle data are synchronized to nearby clients for a small targeted HUD
  label within roughly two blocks; no area scan, chat output or new screen is
  used.
- Tasting Service cup feet and bottle bases were lowered to the tray's inner
  surface. Wood, linen, empty/partial/full cup and red/white state generation
  continues through the existing baked-model path; gameplay hitboxes and
  serving logic are unchanged.
- The full furniture set was audited. Only the three full-collision machine
  blocks that exposed inappropriate sturdy side faces—Aging Barrel,
  Fermentation Barrel and Grape Press—needed a narrow support-shape override.
  Their top/bottom support and gameplay collision remain intact. The other
  audited furniture already used inset or shallow shapes.
- Same-facing Estate Management Desks now expose left/right connection states
  across wood variants. Sneak placement stores a persistent standalone state;
  normal rows update when neighbours are added or removed. Desk block entities,
  maps, ownership, reports and payloads remain independent.

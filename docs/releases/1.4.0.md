# Vintner 1.4.0 — Vineyard Expansion

Publication draft: manual release acceptance is still required. Do not publish this candidate yet.

Vineyard Expansion brings more character to your vines and gives your estate the tools to understand its harvests, cellars and progress. Vineyard and winery work still happens in the world; the new management tools help you plan and inspect it.

## Eight cultivars, more vineyard decisions

- Grow Ember Noir, Vale Pinot, Suncrest and River Garnet reds, or Golden Vale, Frostling, Honeycrest and Stoneflower whites. Cultivar identity follows the grapes through the wine-making process.
- Develop vines over time with persistent vine age and its yield/quality trade-offs.
- Choose Balanced, Quality Focus or High Yield management for your vineyard.
- Propagate cuttings in Nursery Beds, graft cultivars and compare own-root, adapted and resistant rootstock strategies.

## Read and protect your site

- Assess soil, climate and terrain with the Soil Probe and contextual vineyard guidance.
- Respond to drought, frost, heat, rot, mildew, nutrient imbalance, pests and birds.
- Prepare vineyard soil, protect vines with netting and use nearby water channels for irrigation.
- Meet the Winemaker and Cooper village professions, supported by grape presses, barrel stands and village vineyard content.

## Build an estate you can manage

- Register an estate through the Almanac and a Vintage Archive, with optional custom naming.
- Define up to sixteen named vineyard plots, each up to 32 × 32 blocks, with dimension-aware bounds and overlap protection.
- Follow estate activity through a persistent ledger, reputation and recognition of physical facilities.
- Inspect barrel workshops, controlled cellars, storage, tasting rooms and vineyard irrigation as you develop the estate.
- Open the Estate Management Desk for Overview, Vineyards, Cellar, Markets, Ledger and Map reports.
- Pair the desk with a nearby Surveyor's Map Table and up to nine compatible explored maps for Estate Atlas selection, panning, zoom and vineyard overlays.
- Compare wine appraisal, buyer fit and regional market guidance. These reports are read-only: this release does not add sales, contracts, currency or rewards from trading.

## Stability and reporting

- Estate reports preserve owner isolation and static plot information when live conditions are unavailable.
- Loaded-plot reporting performs less repeated irrigation work while keeping reports fresh and synchronous.
- Expanded automated coverage checks plots, rejected overlaps, dimension separation, estate reporting and facility/reputation persistence.
- Existing pressing, fermentation, ageing, bottles, servings, cellar furniture, Almanac inspection and Vintage Archives remain Vintner-owned systems.

## Display and decorative polish

- Craft eight matching cultivar grapes with a wooden bowl to make a placeable Grape Bowl, then eat its four servings or preserve a partially eaten bowl as décor.
- Wine Baskets cradle one real Vintner bottle diagonally, preserve its exact identity and contents, and return it safely when retrieved or broken.
- Trellis rows can follow one-block terrain changes, with sloped wires updating when endpoints are added or removed.
- Aged red and white wine now has a distinct gold-finished visual across placed bottles, racks, crates, cellar displays and Tasting Services while preserving the wine's metadata.
- Wine Racks, Wine Crates and placed wine bottles now use one consistent red/white visual language, including a mixed display for storage containing both styles.
- Looking directly at a placed wine bottle from close range shows its existing player-facing wine name without opening a screen or posting chat messages.
- Bottles and cups now sit naturally on Tasting Service trays across wood, linen and serving-state variants.
- Barrel-like machines and related cellar furniture no longer attract visually inappropriate fence, wall or pane connections.
- Estate Management Desks join into continuous same-facing rows across wood variants. Sneak while placing to keep a desk visually standalone; each desk remains independently functional.

## Installation and compatibility

- Minecraft Java Edition **26.2**, **Java 25**, and **Fabric Loader 0.19.3 or later compatible with 26.2**.
- **Fabric API** is required; the tested version is **0.158.0+26.2**.
- **Zenithgb Commons 0.1.0** is required separately on clients and servers. Its technical mod ID is `zenithgb_library`; it is not bundled inside Vintner.
- Install Vintner and its required dependencies on both client and server. Neither Tilth nor Reeve is required.
- Commons provides shared reference infrastructure and Vintner reference articles. The Almanac, Estate Management Desk and Vintage Archives retain their specialist roles.
- Serene Seasons is optional; use the Fabric build compatible with Minecraft 26.2 and its required GlitchCore dependency. Final candidate validation is recorded separately in the release manifest.
- Back up existing worlds before upgrading. Test a copy first, including wine metadata, partial servings, machines, estates, maps and stored inventories. Do not downgrade an upgraded save.

Recipe-viewer integration, recipe redesigns, pantry/cooking, transactional markets and further estate, settlement or logistics features are outside this release.

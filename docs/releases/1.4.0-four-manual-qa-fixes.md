## Corrected behavior

- Creative Pick Block now returns the exact displayed wine stack only when the
  occupied Tasting Service bottle itself is targeted. The server rechecks
  Creative mode, reach, block, hit region and current contents; tray/stand,
  empty and Survival cases retain normal behavior. The source service is not
  mutated.
- Wine Rack, Wine Crate, Tasting Cabinet and Labelled Cellar Shelf displays now
  render their actual synchronized bottle slots. The nearby identification HUD
  resolves the exact facing-aware visible slot and clears on empty gaps,
  furniture surfaces, content changes or unrelated targets. Placed bottles and
  Wine Baskets retain their existing identification paths.
- Ordinary red, ordinary white, aged red and aged white now share a consistent
  generated palette across placed bottles, storage displays, Tasting Services
  and Wine Baskets. Aged bottles retain the restrained gold finish; ordinary
  red and white are no longer visually collapsed into the aged palette.
- The Nursery Bed inventory/held source is a dedicated empty Blockbench model
  with frame, soil and divider only. The exporter no longer derives the item
  from a planted growth state. Placed growth geometry and behavior are
  unchanged.

# Vintner 1.4.0 model manual acceptance

Use the exact committed candidate in a disposable Minecraft 26.3 world. Human
visual checks remain pending until a person marks them complete; automated
build, GameTest or startup evidence does not approve subjective appearance.

## Vineyard

- [ ] Compare isolated, straight, end, corner, junction and hillside Trellis
  runs in Oak, Poplar, Pale Oak, Bamboo, Crimson and Warped.
- [ ] Confirm wire transitions have no visible gaps or Z-fighting on slopes.
- [ ] Inspect young and mature red/white vines at both vertical levels.
- [ ] Inspect all four Nursery ages, empty bed, adjacent beds and the empty
  GUI/hand/ground/fixed item presentation.
- [ ] Confirm Grape Bowl fruit reads as one natural bunch and each eaten state
  removes fruit from the intended end without floating pieces.

## Production

- [ ] Inspect Grape Press, Fermentation Barrel, Aging Barrel and Barrel Stand
  in representative overworld, Poplar and Nether woods.
- [ ] Confirm must/airlock/status overlays sit on the equipment without seams.
- [ ] Confirm Toasted, Seasoned and Cellar Cask upgrades remain visually
  distinct without appearing as separate wood-family registrations.

## Cellar

- [ ] Fill and partially fill a rack, crate, labelled shelf and tasting cabinet
  with mixed red, white, ordinary and aged bottles.
- [ ] Confirm every bottle is supported, individually targetable and free of
  clipping; stack crates and place adjacent racks/shelves.
- [ ] Inspect Archive standalone and connected to both estate modules.

## Serving

- [ ] Inspect empty and occupied Wine Baskets in representative woods and all
  four bottle appearances; confirm the bottle rests in the approved cradle.
- [ ] Inspect empty, partial and full Tasting Services with several linen
  colours and all four bottle appearances in every facing.
- [ ] Confirm bottle, glasses and liquid touch the tray and do not float.

## Estate

- [ ] Build Desk/Map Table/Archive rows in each order, including mixed wood,
  and confirm one continuous desktop with no gaps or doubled edge pieces.
- [ ] Inspect Desk ledger, blotter colours and live map frame.
- [ ] Inspect Map Table stored-map overlay and standalone silhouette.

## Items

- [ ] Review block items in GUI, first/third person, ground and fixed frames.
- [ ] Review Soil Probe, Grafting Knife, Cooper's Mallet and upgrade kits at
  normal GUI and held scales.

## Poplar

- [ ] Place one Poplar example from every supported family and confirm texture
  direction, scale and colour are consistent with the Poplar set.
- [ ] Compare Poplar beside Pale Oak, Bamboo, Crimson and Warped for accidental
  fallback or wrong-family textures.

## Wine bottles

- [ ] Confirm every inventory, held, placed, stored, basket and service bottle
  keeps a dark olive-green glass body.
- [ ] Confirm red versus white and aged versus ordinary remain readable through
  restrained capsule, label, seal and gold accents.
- [ ] Pick Block a placed bottle and Tasting Service and confirm the exact
  metadata/servings are preserved.

## Rendering environments

- [ ] Repeat representative gallery checks with default/no shaders.
- [ ] Repeat representative gallery checks with the existing showcase shader
  configuration if available, noting any clipping, darkness or transparency.

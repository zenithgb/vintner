# Vintner 1.4.0 — green bottles and placed-bottle pick checklist

Use the replacement candidate identified in
`docs/releases/1.4.0-green-bottles-and-placed-pick.md`. These are direct human
observations; leave an item unchecked until it has been seen in Minecraft.

- [ ] Ordinary red, ordinary white, aged red and aged white all read as
  traditional green-glass wine bottles in inventory, held view and the world.
- [ ] Burgundy red-wine and straw-gold/light white-wine accents remain readable
  without whole-body recolouring.
- [ ] Aged red and aged white remain distinguishable from ordinary versions
  without losing their red/white identity.
- [ ] Creative Pick Block on an individually placed bottle returns the exact
  wine, metadata and remaining servings.
- [ ] Repeated placed-bottle picking leaves the original bottle unchanged.
- [ ] Tasting Service wine-bottle pick and normal service/tray pick both work.
- [ ] Storage identification tooltips follow the individually targeted bottle
  in racks, crates, cabinets and labelled cellar shelves.
- [ ] Bottle appearance is acceptable with shaders off and in the normal
  showcase setup, including storage orientations and Wine Baskets.

Broader ordinary-gameplay, real-two-player and copied stable-world upgrade
acceptance remain separate release gates.
